📚 Book & PDF Framework

Grammar Books → Mon/TueOne rule per week. Read the explanation, complete ALL exercises, then write 5 personal examples. If you can't produce it, you haven't learned it.
Vocabulary Books → Tue/WedOne chapter per day. Write each term 3×, then one original sentence. Attach new words to old ones (word families, prefixes).
Workbooks → Thu/Fri2–3 pages timed (20 min max). Every wrong answer gets rewritten correctly 3× — errors are the curriculum.

⚡ The 5 Non-Negotiable Rules

  1. Consistency over intensity — 90 minutes daily beats 10 hours on Sunday.
  2. Output every day — speaking and writing, not just passive input.
  3. Embrace errors — each mistake corrected is permanently installed knowledge.
  4. Learn chunks, not words — "ich würde vorschlagen, dass…" not isolated vocabulary.
  5. Track visibly — the dashboard's checked boxes are your contract with yourself.
